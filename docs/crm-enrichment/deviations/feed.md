# Deviations — Feed (Phase 2 screen build)

Where the Feed screen (`Feed.dc.html`: Feed, Scoring models, Enrichment) departs from the design, and why.
Same columns as `DEVIATIONS.md`; the lead builder folds them in.

| ID | Where | Design | Built | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| V-FEED-01 | Feed, «✓ N done today» | `4 + done.length` | `feed_progress.done_today`: my cards resolved today by task, email or done | FAB in the spec |
| V-FEED-02 | Feed › progress, Today | Tasks 5/8, Calls 3/6, Activities n/14 | My activities starting today, done / total, per type (task, call) and all; an empty bar at 0/0 | FAB in the spec |
| V-FEED-03 | Feed › progress, This month | New deals 14, Won 6 · 312 000 kr, Forecast 726 100 kr | `feed_progress.month`: my deals created this month, won this month (count and sum), Σ value × stage probability of my open deals closing this month | FAB in the spec |
| V-FEED-04 | Feed › progress, This month | «Goal: won value 62 % of 500 000 kr» | Left out | Needs goals (INS-05, phase 4) |
| V-FEED-05 | Feed, every card | Fixed English prose | Worded from the card's kind and parameters through messages; «Email reply» shows the subject, «readiness n / 10» only when an insight exists and AI scoring is on | Cards are kind + params (0022); the AI summary does not exist (AI not configured) |
| V-FEED-06 | Feed › Planned today | «Call Julie — Org», «14:00 · follow-up call · high priority» | «{activity subject} — {org}», «{time} · {type} · {priority} priority» from the activity | FAB in the spec |
| V-FEED-07 | Feed › Email opened | «Last open 25 min ago from {city}» | «Last open {relative time}»; no place | The city is the organisation's, not the open's (FAB) |
| V-FEED-08 | Feed › Sequence email waiting | One card «2 sequence emails … and 1 more» | One card per message awaiting review: ««subject» is waiting for manual send», «Sequence «name» · org» | 0022 makes one card per message |
| V-FEED-10 | Feed › Stalled | «In Demo for 3 weeks — deals like this close 40 % less often after 21 days» | «In {stage} for {days} days» | The statistic is not computed from history (FAB) |
| V-FEED-11 | Feed › Likely to advance | «Likely to advance — 72 %» | The percentage only when the card's params carry one; no such cards exist while AI is not configured; hidden when `ai_scoring` is off (`feed_get`) | FAB; AIA-02 |
| V-FEED-12 | Feed › Cross-sell signal | «120 shift workers · Skiftly fits · create an expansion deal» | «{employees} employees · one application · create an expansion deal»; «Schedule task» disabled (the card has no deal); «Open» shows the organization with its first three activities and «Open organization →» | FAB; V-18 rule; built in the design's language (D-41) |
| V-FEED-14 | Feed › Suggestions | AI cards, slipping, cross-sell, stalled | `new_no_activity`, `cross_sell`, `likely_to_advance` (slipping and stalled are Overlooked in 0022) | PRO-01 «Opportunities» folded into Suggestions (spec §2) |
| V-FEED-15 | Feed › Email | Marks the card done, «Composer opened» | Opens the inbox and resolves the card only when a mailbox can send (`mailboxes_get`: synced/syncing and can_reply); otherwise a toast says no mailbox can send yet, and the card stays | No mailbox is connected (D-41) |
| V-FEED-16 | Feed, sub-tab row | No filters | A «Filters» button: card type, stage, a deal-score range while AI scoring is on (`feed_get` filter, 0027), and the pipeline scope (`feed_scope_set`) | PRO-01/02, built in the design's language (D-41); the score range is hidden when `ai_scoring` is off, as scores are |
| V-FEED-17 | Feed, narrow screens | Two columns at every width | One column below 1024 px, the progress panel and side panel after the cards (Open scrolls to it); card buttons wrap under the text below 640 px | No horizontal page scroll at phone width |
| V-FEED-18 | Feed, card bar colour | Per card in the prototype | The design's colour per kind (`KIND_TONE`); `feed_card_kinds.tone` is not used | The stored tones (info/warn/good/ai) do not match the design's colours |
| V-FEED-19 | Scoring › model list | Fixed names and subtitles | `score_models_get`: name, «Deals · {pipeline} pipeline · active», «Leads · draft»; «Score models: unlimited (setting)» or «{used} of {limit} (setting)» from `count_limit` | Never fabricate |
| V-FEED-20 | Scoring › criterion rows | Join column IF / OR / AND | «IF» on every row | Each criterion adds its group's points on its own (V-19); AND/OR live inside a criterion's condition |
| V-FEED-21 | Scoring | «Organization health score ≥ 70» criterion; «Health score from Customers is one criterion — no parallel score is built.» | Not offered; the footnote reads «… becomes a criterion when health scores exist — no parallel score is built.» | Health scores arrive in phase 4 (seed V-26) |
| V-FEED-22 | Scoring › Preview | `clamp(30 + Σ)` from heuristics; the first 12 deals | `score_preview` (base 0, V-19) for the first twelve open deals of the model's pipeline (`deal_list`) or open leads (`rec_list`); the record in the address | FAB; base 0 |
| V-FEED-23 | Scoring › «＋ Criterion» | No handler | A field picker over the tests `app.wf_match` makes: email opened / reply received / no reply in N days, participant with a person label (deals), applications in use, payment status, source, value range, employees range, city / county / industry contains; the label is saved in the staff member's language. Added in the design's language: × to remove a criterion, «Activate» / «Set to draft», «＋ Model», «Delete» for a draft model after a confirmation (`score_model_delete`), a «Base · N records scored» line. Changes need deals:configure (disabled otherwise) | D-41 |
| V-FEED-24 | Enrichment › Credits | «618 left», bar, «of 1 000 · resets 1 Nov» | «{n} used» this month from `credit_ledger`, «unlimited (setting) · resets {next 1st} · counts contact-data lookups»; «left» and the bar only when `count_limits` sets an allowance | FAB; D-17 |
| V-FEED-25 | Enrichment › Credits | «Buy top-up →» | Left out | V-23 / D-17 |
| V-FEED-26 | Enrichment › Sources | Provider listed as if working | «… (adapter) · not connected» while `contact_data` is not connected | D-41 |
| V-FEED-27 | Enrichment › Bulk | «Select up to 100 rows in any people or organization list → «Enrich».»; «Enrich 24 organizations with gaps» | «Up to 100 organizations with an org.nr and empty fields per request.»; «Enrich {candidates} organizations with gaps» (`enrichment_get.candidates`; «100 of N» above 100) sends `enrichment_get.candidate_ids` (the first hundred, 0027) to `enrich_request`; the toast says the worker fetches them | FAB; no list offers «Enrich» yet; no worker runs locally, so requests stay «requested» |
| V-FEED-28 | Enrichment › Company enrichment | Mjøsa Energi: address, industry, employees, revenue, LinkedIn, website; «Apply 5 fields · 1 credit» | The chosen request (`enrichment_get.requests`, a select when there are several): Brønnøysund's address, postcode, city, NACE, industry, employees, website; filled fields «(kept)» and not selectable; «Apply N fields» without a credit; «Dismiss»; states requested (waiting for the register), failed, nothing new, none | FAB; revenue needs Regnskapsregisteret, LinkedIn the provider (D-39); Brønnøysund costs no credit |
