Where the build differs from the drawing, and why. The element-by-element map of sources is `../growth-v2-gap.md`;
the earlier Growth deviations (V-SGR-*) stay in force where not replaced here.

## The SEO suite (SeoSuite.dc.html, 0074)

| # | Where | Design | Build | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| V-GR2-23 | SEO sub-tabs | Nine views, SEO overview first | All nine (V-GR2-18 is done): SEO overview, Topic clusters, Opportunities, Keywords, Content plan, Internal links, Search results and competitors, Change log, Alerts; the scope bar only on Keywords, as drawn | — |
| V-GR2-24 | SEO overview KPIs | Organic clicks, organic signups, organic MRR won, traffic value, visibility, indexed pages | Five: organic clicks (Search Console's day totals, 30 days), organic signups (the tracker's search visits that finished signup), organic MRR won (subscriptions confirmed in 30 days on accounts whose first touch was organic search; «—» with «Not reported by the product» while no account carries a source, as Orgpuls's `org.attribution` sends only how a person heard), visibility (the click share the tracked searches' positions earn against holding #1, at the expected click rate per position, in points against the 30 days before), indexed pages (URL Inspection: indexed of inspected, pages dropped out in seven days as the change, the rest «excluded»). «Traffic value» is left out | Traffic value needs cost-per-click data from an ads or SEO tool |
| V-GR2-25 | Opportunities, Top 5 actions | Ten typed rows with Difficulty (KD), «Featured snippet», volumes in searches | Five kinds from Search Console's last 28 days: striking distance (positions 4–20, to the top 3's click rate), low CTR (top 3 below 70 % of the expected rate), content gap (no page in the top 20, to position 5's rate), cannibalization (a search two pages split, assumed to climb one place when merged) and decay (a page's clicks down a quarter against the same 28 days three months before). Value = extra clicks a month × the design's chance × signups per click (the tracker's organic signups ÷ Search Console's clicks since the tracker started, at most 90 days) × paying per account (365 days) × MRR per paying account. Without all three, the value column shows the extra clicks a month and the list is ranked by them. No Difficulty column; no «Featured snippet» kind. Volume is impressions | KD and snippet ownership need an SEO tool's index; Search Console has no search volume |
| V-GR2-26 | Topic clusters | Hand-named topics, MRR per topic, competitors' share of clicks | A topic is the page Google shows most for a set of tracked searches over 90 days, named by its title; visibility week by week over twelve weeks, its level over the last four and the change against the first four in points; «{n} clicks · 90 d» instead of MRR; supporting pages are the topic's other ranking pages (Published), the plan's items (In plan) and its searches with no page in the top 20 (Missing, «Plan»); the share block splits the topic's clicks between the site's own pages | No landing page is reported with an account, so MRR cannot be assigned to a topic; competitors' clicks need SERP data |
| V-GR2-27 | Content plan and brief | Values typed in; owners by first name; «Length vs top 3»; four measured tiles incl. Signups and Value | A card's value is the current estimate of its search's opportunity («—» once there is none); owners are the staff's names; reaching Published logs the change once and keeps the date it is measured from; 28 days after, Search Console's clicks and position before and after for the page (or, for a new page without an address, the search). The brief's related searches and questions are Search Console's own queries sharing a word with the target; internal links to add are crawled pages that mention it without linking. No «Length vs top 3»; tiles for clicks and position only | Result-page length needs SERP data; signups per article need a landing page on the account |
| V-GR2-28 | Internal links | Six hand-made suggestions | From the crawler's last whole crawl: a page whose content (not navigation, header or footer) mentions a tracked or frequent search but does not link to the page Google shows most for it. «Add link» records the decision and logs the change; Sentral does not edit the website, and the next crawl that finds the link retires the suggestion. Orphans: crawled pages no other page links to, with their visits; most linked pages by distinct linking pages and their commonest anchor | A suggestion must rest on what the site actually says and links |
| V-GR2-29 | Search results and competitors | Visibility against competitors, result features, keyword gaps | One card saying what the view needs (an SEO tool: Ahrefs, Semrush or DataForSEO) and why Search Console cannot fill it | No source; nothing is drawn that would look like data |
| V-GR2-30 | Change log | Six sample changes, oldest first; «No effect» for any drop | Real changes, newest first, from three places (by hand, the plan, «Add link»); clicks, position and CTR over the 28 days after against the 28 before, once Search Console has reported them («Measuring» until then, «—» without a baseline); a drop of 10 % or more is «Loss» (the design's peach and rust) | A fall labelled «No effect» would misstate it |
| V-GR2-31 | Alerts | Seven rules; channels In app, Email, Slack, Ticket; sample history | Four rules with a source: ranking drop (a top-10 tracked search three places down in Search Console's last seven days, ten impressions each week, once a week), page dropped from the index (URL Inspection), organic traffic drop (the tracker's search visits in the last hour 30 % under the same hour on the same weekday, averaged over up to four weeks, when that hour usually brings five), Core Web Vitals fail (PageSpeed's mobile LCP over 2,5 s or INP over 200 ms on a measured page, once in 28 days). Checked hourly; delivered in the app and by email (through Brevo, when mail is ready) to everybody who may read Growth's reports. Rule changes need Campaigns › Edit others', acknowledging Campaigns › Add | «Lost featured snippet», «Lost backlink» and «Competitor jump» need SERP and backlink data; Slack has no sender in Sentral; a support ticket is for customers and would count in support's figures |
| V-GR2-32 | Index coverage | Search Console's coverage report | URL Inspection per crawled page (at most once in 20 hours, after the nightly import, within Google's 2 000 a day); the buckets are Google's coverage states reduced to the design's names, plus the states it does not draw | The coverage report has no API; URL Inspection does |

## The playbook (GrowthPlaybook.dc.html, 0076)

| # | Where | Design | Build | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| V-GR2-33 | Outcomes and attribution | Four models (first, last, position-based, data-driven); «Paid status is sent back to Google Ads, LinkedIn and Meta as a conversion»; organic CAC «content cost» | First and last touch (`am` in the address); 90 days of spend (typed in per month, the share of each month inside the window), the tracker's signups per channel, accounts and paying customers by the product's first or last touch, MRR of the subscriptions confirmed in the window; CAC = spend ÷ paying, payback = CAC ÷ ARPA (today's MRR ÷ paying accounts) in the design's colours. Without a source in any account's attribution, accounts, paying, CAC, payback, MRR and the journeys are «—» and a line says why; a channel without spend has «—» for its costs. Journeys run first touch → last touch (when it differs) → Signup, with the paying count and the median days from signup to paying. The right-hand note says where spend comes from | The product reports two touches, not the path between them; nothing is sent to an ad platform; a content cost is not recorded |
| V-GR2-34 | Budget planner | Hand-set budgets; «per paying, next 10k» (marginal cost); «Target 3 500 kr» on blended CAC; a fixed move | A budget per channel and month (`marketing_budgets`, the six spend channels), typed into the row and saved on leaving the field; pace against the share of the month gone (±15 %, −20 %, the day it runs out at today's rate); «per paying, 90 days» is the cost of a paying customer each channel showed over 90 days, and the projection is the budget at that cost. The suggested move appears when the dearest channel's cost is half again the cheapest's: 30 % of the dearest's budget, in whole hundreds; «Apply move» moves it in one step and the month remembers it («✓ Applied»). The scenario buys at the cheapest observed cost and prices it at ARPA. Blended CAC carries «Spend ÷ paying customers, 90 days» instead of a target. Setting a budget needs Campaigns › Reports on others' | A marginal cost needs bid-level data; no target is recorded |
| V-GR2-35 | Experiments | Lift, confidence, sample progress on running tests; «Call result» decides from the lift; «goal 4–8 tests a month» | Hypotheses with area and I/C/E chosen in the add bar (`growth_experiments`); Backlog → Ready → Running with ← and →; a running test shows «Running since … · day n»; «Call result» records Win, No effect or Loss with a line on what was seen; Done → ← reopens it; «Archive» takes it off the board. The pace line counts running tests and tests called in 30 days | Lift and confidence need an A/B testing tool; no goal is recorded |
| V-GR2-36 | Audiences | Audiences synced daily to Google Ads, LinkedIn and Meta with match rates; results by persona | The CRM's lists (segments_get) with who matches and who can receive email, and whose they are; one line says no ad platform is connected. Results by persona is one card saying the product does not report the signup's role | Syncing needs ad platform APIs; persona needs a role on the signup |
| V-GR2-37 | Creative and landing pages | Message matrix, ad variants with fatigue, landing page A/B tests | One card saying what the view needs (ad platform figures, a testing tool) and where landing pages are measured today (Pages and links) | No source; nothing is drawn that would look like data |
| V-GR2-38 | Calendar and offers | Search-interest row; hand-placed bars; offers table; referral and partners with a programme switch | The year's ad campaigns by channel (their dates, or from creation to today), the email campaigns sent or scheduled (a day, drawn at least 28 px wide), blackout periods (`growth_blackouts`) people add and remove under the plan; the year in the corner; the current month in ink. One card says offers, coupons and partner referrals have no record | Search interest needs Google Trends (no API); Sentral holds no offers or partner referrals |
| V-GR2-39 | Trial and abandoned signup | Trial → paid with flow vs control; recovered signups; branches with goals and reach; control-group select; recovery toggle and email steps | Trial → paid over the trials that ended in 90 days, in points against the 90 before; median days from account to first survey (activation milestones); abandoned signups are the tracker's visits that began the signup and did not finish it in 30 days; «Recovered» «—». The flows are the workflows a product event starts (an account created, a milestone reached, billing changed), with their steps (✉ the email's name, ◷ the delay, ✓ another step), how many are in them and how many finished in 90 days. No control group. The recovery card says why there is no address to write to and links to the funnel | The engine has no holdout; a visit that stops before the account exists leaves no email address and the tracker is cookieless |
| V-GR2-40 | Flow library | Twelve flows with lift, unsubscribe, MRR and a health grade | Every sequence, lifecycle flow and workflow that writes to people (an email or campaign step), Live / Paused / Draft, its description, steps and kind, people in it, and 90 days of what its campaigns sent (sent, opened, unsubscribed) when it sent anything. Activate and Pause are the engine's own switch; a blocker disables Activate and says why | Lift, MRR per flow and a health grade need a control group and revenue per flow |
| V-GR2-41 | Deliverability and frequency | Inbox placement by provider; domain reputation; frequency cap, quiet hours, priority between flows; four signals | KPIs: sent in 30 days, bounce rate, spam complaints against Google's and Yahoo's 0,3 % limit, unsubscribe rate, sender domains passing SPF, DKIM and DMARC. Sender domains with their checks and bounce rate replace inbox placement; the suppression list by reason, with a true line that the engine has no frequency cap, quiet hours or priority, replaces the settings. The signals are what the engine does: the owner notified on a first open or click, a sequence stopped on a reply, suppressions for unsubscribes, hard bounces and complaints, counted this month | Placement needs seed tests or Postmaster; settings the engine would not enforce would change nothing |

## The ad platforms (0077)

| # | Where | Design | Build | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| V-GR2-42 | Integrations › Google Ads, LinkedIn Ads, Meta Ads | Cards marked «Connected»; Connect/Disconnect | Three real connections, read only. Google Ads (API v25): a developer token and a service account added as a user on the ad account (Search Console's or its own key), an optional manager account. LinkedIn Ads (versioned API 202609): the app's client id and secret, then «Sign in with LinkedIn»; ads-oauth exchanges the code itself and keeps the tokens in Vault, renewing them with LinkedIn's refresh token where the app is approved for it. Meta Ads (Graph v26.0): a system user's token, and the app secret for appsecret_proof when the app asks for it. Every credential goes to secret-intake after a fresh second factor; the owner links each ad account to an application; «Import now», «Check again», «Disconnect». A nightly job (05:07 UTC) imports each linked account's campaigns, ads and days, a week back for late conversions, ninety days the first time | Sentral changes nothing on a platform: pausing, budgets, audiences and conversions stay in the platform |
| V-GR2-43 | Spend | Spend from the platforms | A platform campaign counts as a Sentral campaign: the one carrying its utm_campaign (from Google's tracking template or final URL suffix, Meta's url_tags), one chosen by hand (Campaigns › Edit others'), or one made for it once it runs or has activity. Its whole months replace typed spend (a month imported only in part keeps what was typed); typing over an imported month is refused; spend is recomputed from what is imported on every import and link, so it never counts twice. The campaign's state follows the platform and its Pause button is off. Spend in another currency than NOK is kept but not counted in kroner | One source per month; no currency conversion is invented |
| V-GR2-44 | Creative and landing pages | Message matrix, ad variants with fatigue and Refresh, landing page tests | With a platform connected: «Ad variants», each ad's click-through over the last seven days against the seven before, fatigued when it fell 30 % or more with at least 1 000 impressions in each week; frequency (impressions per person reached a day, Meta) is shown, not judged. «Platform campaigns» with 30 days of impressions, clicks, CTR, spend and conversions and the Sentral campaign each counts as. No Refresh button; no message matrix or landing page tests (supersedes V-GR2-37 where a platform is connected) | Refresh would write to the platform; the matrix needs ads tagged by pain point and persona, and tests need an A/B tool; a week's reach is not the sum of its days |
| V-GR2-45 | Audiences | Lists synced daily to Google Ads, LinkedIn and Meta | Not synced, connected or not; the card says so | Uploading contacts' addresses (hashed) to an ad platform is a decision about personal data nobody has made |
| V-GR2-46 | Outcomes › «Paid status is sent back as a conversion» | Conversions sent back | Not sent (V-GR2-33 stands) | Needs a click id (gclid, li_fat_id, fbclid) captured at signup and reported by the product, and write access |
| V-GR2-47 | SEO › Keywords | A list of keywords, each with the query and a status line | Every search Google showed the site for in the period (the 200 with most impressions; a note says when there are more) and the keywords tracked by hand (0084). Under the status line, the page Google showed most for it and «tracked» for a tracked one; a tracked head term Google never reports alone says how many longer searches contain it. The detail offers «Track» for a search nobody tracks | Tor, 12 Oct: «Shouldn't we automatically track our search words on the pages and which pages?» — the list held only hand-tracked keywords, so 28 real searches were invisible and «arbeidsmiljø» read «No impressions yet» |
| V-GR2-48 | SEO › Overview, SEO › Keywords, Pages › the page card | Not drawn: countries, devices, a page's own figures from Google | SEO › Overview has two cards under the actions and index coverage, in the suite's table style: «Countries» (the ten seen most in Google search over 30 days, the rest as «n other countries», a country Google could not place as «Unknown») and «Devices» (desktop, mobile, tablet), each with clicks, impressions, the change in impressions against the 30 days before, click rate and position. The country is named in the reader's language from Google's ISO code. Keywords ends its list with how many impressions and clicks in the period came from rare searches Search Console counts but does not name. The page card says the page's impressions, clicks and position in Google search over the keywords' period, and how much of that came from rare searches its keyword chips cannot show. Before the first import that reads them, the cards say they arrive with the next import (0091) | Tor, 14 Oct: «should we include impressions, clicks and every information we get from Google Search» — yes to a page's own figures and to countries and devices. A page summed from its named searches showed 122 of 241 impressions and none of the 3 clicks. Search appearance and the Image, Video, News and Discover search types are left out: at about 240 impressions a fortnight there is nothing to show |
| V-GR2-49 | SEO › Keywords › «Do this» below position 20 | «Not competitive yet: create a dedicated landing page — the current page targets a different intent» for every search below 20 | The design's advice once the position has settled. Before that: a search Google shows several pages for asks for one page first (`low_split`), and one tracked for under eight weeks says it is too early to judge, with links to earn meanwhile (`low_young`, `KW_SETTLE_DAYS`). A search with no tracking date keeps the design's advice | Tor, 18 Oct, on «leadership team effectiveness» at 58.5 four days after LeaderPact was indexed: the advice read no intent and no age, and pointed at a second page that would compete with the right one (LeaderPact D-67) |
