# Deviations — Growth v2 (SimpleGrowth.dc.html, SeoSuite.dc.html, GrowthPlaybook.dc.html of 11 Oct 2026)

Where the build differs from the drawing, and why. The element-by-element map of sources is `../growth-v2-gap.md`;
the earlier Growth deviations (V-SGR-*) stay in force where not replaced here.

| # | Where | Design | Build | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| V-GR2-01 | Tabs, scope bar | Buttons with component state | Links: the tab, period, day, hour, source, device and every pick live in the address (`lib/growth/scope.ts`); the old `?tab=analytics` opens the Overview | A drill-down or a filter can be linked to and survives a reload |
| V-GR2-02 | SEO › Keywords scope bar | Today, 7, 30, 90 days; Source; Device | 7, 30 and 90 days; no Source or Device | Search Console has no sources or devices in its import and lags two days; a filter that changes nothing is not offered |
| V-GR2-03 | Account figures under a device filter | Filtered like everything | «—» with the reason as a hint (Accounts, Paying, Cost per paying, funnel steps 5–8) | An account has no device; the tracker is never joined to an account (D-37) |
| V-GR2-04 | Cost per paying | Pro-rated spend, «−9 %» | Spend entered per month, spread evenly over its days; none for one day or hour; a change only when both periods have spend | Spend is typed in per month |
| V-GR2-05 | Chart inner bar | Signups inside every bar | Only on Visitors (signups begun ÷ visitors, ×8 as drawn); hidden on the other metrics | The prototype's inner bar on the other metrics is a fixed 60 % filler |
| V-GR2-06 | Biggest opportunities | Six hand-written opportunities | Rules on real figures: a form error (its stopped visits × the step's continue rate), a striking-distance keyword (potential clicks × the site's rate), a page below the site's rate, mobile below desktop, a slow page (0,4 % of its visits, the design's allowance); per month; shown only when their inputs exist | Never an invented estimate |
| V-GR2-07 | Channels | Organic, Paid, Direct, LinkedIn, Referral, Email, Social | channel_rules' channels: Organic search, Paid search, Direct, Social, Referral (new in 0073), Email, Paid social, Partner | A visit's channel comes from its utm and referrer by rule |
| V-GR2-08 | Funnel steps | «Clicked «Kom i gang»», «Created account and verified email», a drop between steps 4 and 5 | «Clicked to sign up» (a press, or the signup page opened); «Created account»; no drop shown between 4 and 5 | Steps 1–4 count visitors, 5–8 accounts: the units differ, and email verification is not reported |
| V-GR2-09 | Why they stop | Survey-style reasons | The pages the stopped visits ended on, and the signup form's error codes; nothing for account steps | Only what the tracker saw |
| V-GR2-10 | Fix | A written fix per step | A rule over the card's own figures (a device gap, a form error, one exit page, a weak channel); no box when no signal is strong enough | A claim must rest on the figures shown |
| V-GR2-11 | Signup log | The last day's sessions; trail to «invoice.paid»; Paying filter | The scope's newest 100 visits (25 at a time, «Show more»); the trail of pages and presses; «Finished signup» instead of «Paying»; the Source column is the channel, the referrer its hover | A visit is not joined to an account (D-37) |
| V-GR2-12 | Referring sites | Links, DR, type, status from a backlink index | Domain, visits, signups, status (New / Active / Lost from referral visits); the line under the domain is its channel | No backlink index (Ahrefs/Semrush/Majestic API) |
| V-GR2-13 | Links and buttons | Human labels | The press's key, made readable («kom-i-gang-hero» → «Kom i gang hero»); CTR = visits that pressed ÷ visits that saw the page | The site sends a key, never a label |
| V-GR2-14 | Page card | Recommendation texts about images, calculators and templates | The same four rules with neutral wording; LCP, INP and CLS from PageSpeed Insights (INP only from real-user data) | No claim about a page's content the platform has not read |
| V-GR2-15 | SEO › Keywords | Volume; «Who ranks above us»; Δ 30d | Impressions in the Volume column (V-SGR-08); no result-page list; Δ over the chosen period; intent by rule; «Stop tracking» in the card (V-SGR-07) | Search Console has no search volume and no competitors; result-page data needs a SERP API |
| V-GR2-16 | Technical SEO | Six sample issues, «crawled today 04:00» | The crawl's issues (V-SGR-31) plus PageSpeed's slow pages and Search Console's split searches (no «Mark fixed»: they clear with the figures); the header has the real crawl time; a footer with «Check now» (V-SGR-32) | Real sources only |
| V-GR2-17 | Content gaps | «6 200 searches/month» | Impressions over 28+ days (V-SGR-30) | No search volume |
| V-GR2-18 | SEO sub-tabs | Nine views | The bar appears once two views exist; Keywords first, the SEO suite (SeoSuite.dc.html) follows | A bar with one choice offers none |
| V-GR2-19 | Campaign and Email sub-tabs | Seven and four views | As V-GR2-18, with GrowthPlaybook.dc.html | Same |
| V-GR2-20 | Integrations | Seventeen cards, mostly «Connected», Connect/Disconnect | The six Sentral has, each with its true state: website tracker, site crawler («Check now»), Google Search Console and PageSpeed Insights («Connect» / «Manage» opens their setup below), the product's events, Brevo | GA4, Google Ads, GTM, BigQuery, Trends, Business Profile, Looker Studio, Ahrefs/Semrush, Bing, Clarity/Hotjar, LinkedIn, Meta, Microsoft Ads, Stripe and Slack have no connector here |
| V-GR2-21 | Sync log | Seven sample lines | Twelve newest: imports, crawls and PageSpeed runs (growth_sync_log), the products' events by hour, the tracker's visits by day | Every line is a real run |
| V-GR2-22 | Paid search terms | — | The ads' utm_term table that Growth › Analytics carried (0068) is not in the new design; it stays in Analytics › Web and returns with Outcomes and attribution | The design has no place for it yet |
