# Sentral Simple › Growth — departures from the design

Design: `design-reference/sentral-simple/SimpleGrowth.dc.html` and `GrowthMarketing.dc.html` (views `campaigns`, `email`). Route
`/growth?tab=analytics|seo|campaigns|email&range=7d|30d|90d`. Migrations `0058_simple_growth.sql` (overview, SEO, marketing
campaigns) and `0060_simple_sequences.sql` (the email editor on the workflow engine), proved by
`supabase/tests/simple_growth_invariants.sql`.

| ID | Where | Design | Built | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| V-SGR-01 | Tab pills, range control | Buttons that change local state | Links (`?tab=`, `&range=`), styled as the buttons; a tab the person may not open is not offered (Analytics/SEO: a reports right; Campaigns/Email: Campaigns › See) | Control substitution: the prototype has no router; a view needs an address |
| V-SGR-02 | Analytics › KPIs «Visitors», «Visit → signup»; chart | 21 480 visitors, 1,5 % | «—» (tooltip: visitors appear once the website tracker runs on orgpuls.com); the chart draws signups only, with that line under it, until the tracker sends sessions | No web tracker ingest exists (D-37); never a number |
| V-SGR-03 | Analytics › KPI deltas | A delta on every tile | Signups (and visitors once tracked) compare with the period before; «Trial → paid» and «Cost per customer» carry none | Trial → paid is counted by monthly cohorts (as Analytics › Business) and spend is entered per month: a 7- or 30-day «before» would overlap |
| V-SGR-04 | Analytics › Funnel | Six figures | «Visited the site» needs the tracker («—»); «Viewed pricing» and «Started signup» have no definition or source («—»); the bottom three follow the accounts created in the period (first survey from `activation_milestones`, paying from `subscriptions`) | No source for the top; a cohort keeps the bottom monotonic |
| V-SGR-05 | Analytics › «Where visitors come from», «Top landing pages» | Visits, signups and conversion per source and per page | Signups by the first touch's channel (real); visits «—» while the tracker is silent; landing pages empty (the tracker's line); signups and conversion per page «—» always | A cookieless session cannot be joined to a signup |
| V-SGR-06 | SEO › «Indexed pages», «Site health», «Content ideas» | KPI, four issues with «Mark fixed», three ideas with «Plan article» | Superseded by V-SGR-29..32 (0072): all three are built | — |
| V-SGR-07 | SEO › Tracked keywords | Add only | A × per row stops tracking (own keywords with Campaigns › Add; others' with Edit others) | `seo_keyword_remove`; a list that only grows is not usable |
| V-SGR-08 | SEO › KPIs and columns | Clicks, impressions, position; «Volume»; «Ranking page» | Superseded by V-SGR-12 for the connect card. The «Volume» column shows Search Console impressions; «Ranking page» is the page Google showed most for the query in the period (0072, day × query × page import), «Not ranking yet» once connected without impressions, otherwise «—»; positions «—» until connected | Search Console has no search volume |
| V-SGR-09 | Analytics › «Cost per customer» | 1 840 kr | Spend typed in for this application's marketing campaigns in the calendar months the period touches ÷ new paying customers of those months; «—» without spend (tooltip says which months) | Spend is entered per month and is never pro-rated |
| V-SGR-10 | Campaigns › New campaign modal | Landing page select of four paths; no dates | Landing page is a path input (default «/»); «Starts» and «Ends» date inputs; channel «Other» besides the design's five | There is no source of the site's pages; an end date is how a campaign ends (V-SGR-15); `ad_campaigns.channel` allows other |
| V-SGR-11 | Campaigns › Tracking link | `utm_medium` paid / email / event; host orgpuls.com | Medium per `channel_rules` (Google Ads cpc, LinkedIn/Facebook paid_social, Newsletter email, Webinar event, Other referral); host = the application's public site (`web_sites.product`, 0058; orgpuls.com is Orgpuls's); «Copy link» copies it | So the signups a link brings land under the campaign's channel in Analytics |
| V-SGR-12 | Campaigns › Figures | Spent, visits, signups invented | Spent = what was typed in under the utm_campaign; visits «—» without the tracker; signups and paying customers = first touches carrying the utm (0 shown as 0); budget used «—» without a budget; another person's figures need Campaigns › See reports of others or Edit others (V-P6C-19) | Real figures only |
| V-SGR-13 | Campaigns › Pause / Resume / Launch now | Toggles the status | Changes the record only; the card says so under its title, and the toast says to pause/start the ads on the platform | No ad platform is connected |
| V-SGR-14 | Campaigns › Spend | — | A «Spend» row in the campaign card: the months entered and month + amount + «Save spend» (`analytics_set_spend` through the Analytics action, channel from the campaign) | Spend must be typed somewhere, and the old Analytics screen leaves the rail (S5); shown with Campaigns › See reports of others |
| V-SGR-15 | Campaigns › Ended, Duplicate | A status | «Ended» is derived from the end date; «Duplicate» makes a scheduled copy without dates | No ad platform tells us |
| V-SGR-16 | Email › Saving | Edits live in the prototype's state | Each change is saved as it is made (a text field when it is left) through `simple_sequence_save`, one transaction; «＋ New email campaign» creates the campaign at once | The design has no Save button |
| V-SGR-17 | Email › «Starts when» | Seven triggers | «Deal moves to Proposal sent» (the stage of that name) and «Contact fills in a form» work; the other five are disabled options with the reason: account triggers have no single person to email (campaign_action emails a person, a deal's or a lead's contact), there is no trial-end or list-entry trigger, a one-off send is a broadcast campaign | The engine's triggers |
| V-SGR-18 | Email › «Send to list» | Invented list names | The real segments, plus «Anyone the trigger starts»; compiled as a segment test in the trigger (0059 adds it to `wf_match`) | `segments_get` |
| V-SGR-19 | Email › «Stop when» | Three options | Compiled as a condition before each email and task that follows a wait: «Account becomes paying» = the organisation has a paying subscription; «replies» = inbound mail linked to the record since the run began; unsubscribed people are never mailed (D-27) | The engine has no exit for workflows; a condition that fails ends the run |
| V-SGR-20 | Email › Condition step | Free text | A select of what the engine can test: «If the account is not paying yet», «If the employee list is not uploaded», «If the contact is on the list» (with a list); «If the contact opened the last email» is disabled with its reason | Campaign opens are not a workflow condition (`wf_match` tests sales mail) |
| V-SGR-21 | Email › Wait step | Free text «Wait 2 days» | The same field, read back as a whole number of days (1–365); anything else is refused with a toast | The engine's delay is a number |
| V-SGR-22 | Email › Activate / Pause | Toggles | Disabled while something blocks activation, with a one-line reason (no email provider first); a draft that was once active reads «Paused» | `wf_blockers`; the engine has draft and active only |
| V-SGR-23 | Email › Figures | Enrolled, opens, sent/opened/clicked invented | Enrolled = runs started; opens = unique opens ÷ delivered of its emails («—» until something tracked is delivered); per email Sent / opened / clicked from its recipients (0 before any) | Real figures only |
| V-SGR-24 | Email › Email step | The text is the email | The text is the subject of an automated campaign draft named «sequence · subject»; its content is written in Campaigns; an email taken out removes its draft | `campaign_action` contract (0037) |
| V-SGR-25 | Email › Rights | — | Editing needs Deals › Manage workflows (the trigger's scope), Campaigns › Manage workflows and Campaigns › Add (Global › Configure for another person's); otherwise read-only with the reason. Marketing has no Deals › Manage workflows | The engine's and the campaigns' rules |
| V-SGR-26 | Data | — | 0058 adds `web_sites.product` and sets orgpuls.com to Orgpuls | Nothing in the repo tied a site to an application; the link host, the SEO site and the visitors need one |
| V-SGR-27 | 390 px | The prototype has no small layout (it overflows) | Tables scroll inside their cards; the campaign card's four stats are two columns under 640 px; the email editor sits under the list until the content is 574 px wide | Keyboard- and phone-usable without page overflow |
| V-SGR-28 | Ranges of the older reads | — | `analytics_web/business/attribution` keep 30/90/365; `growth_overview` and `seo_get` take 7/30/90 and carry everything the tabs show | One read per tab; the older screens are not changed |
| V-SGR-12 | SEO › Search Console | «Connect Search Console» (an OAuth sign-in) | A Google service account instead of an OAuth sign-in (0064): the owner pastes its JSON key after a fresh second factor (secret-intake, Vault `search_console_key`), gives the account access to the property, and search-console-run maps each site to its property (domain property first), imports day totals and day × query rows (180 days back the first time), and again every night at 04:41 UTC. The KPIs are Search Console's own day totals (`search_console_totals`): per-query rows leave out anonymised queries and would undercount. Connected, a card names the property and the last import; the owner can check again, fetch now, replace the key or disconnect | An OAuth app needs a verified Google consent screen and a public callback Sentral does not have; a service account needs neither and reads only what it is given |
| V-SGR-29 | SEO › «Indexed pages» | «Indexed pages 142 +6» | «Pages in Google»: distinct pages Google showed in search in the period (`search_console_pages`), with the change against the period before; «—» until pages are imported for the period. A hint says what it counts | Google offers no count of indexed pages through its API; this is the nearest figure that can be counted honestly |
| V-SGR-30 | SEO › Content ideas | «6 200 searches/month · no page in top 20» | «638 impressions in 30 days · position 14,2 · /page»: searches Google showed the site for over the last 28+ days, ≥ 3 impressions, weighted position over 10, not tracked; top six. «Plan article» writes `seo_content_plan` (campaigns › add); planned ideas stay with «✓ Planned». The title is the query as Google counted it | Search Console has impressions, not search volume, and knows only searches the site already appears for |
| V-SGR-31 | SEO › Site health | Four sample issues | seo-crawl (edge function) reads the site nightly at 05:13 UTC and on «Check now»: sitemap, up to 150 pages, up to 200 more internal links. Ten kinds (sitemap, page errors, noindex, missing/duplicate title and description, missing H1, slow page > 3 s to last byte from the crawler, broken internal links), one row per kind naming at most five paths. «Mark fixed» hides an issue until the next crawl: still found, it returns marked «found again»; gone, it is resolved. «Slow page» is server timing from the crawler, not mobile rendering | A real crawl of the public site; mobile render timing would need a browser (PageSpeed API) |
| V-SGR-32 | SEO › Site health footer | — | An added footer line: when the site was last checked and how many pages, a failed or partial crawl, issues awaiting re-check, and «Check now» | Without it a person cannot tell a clean site from one never checked |
| V-SGR-33 | Shell header | `min-height:60px` on the border-box header | The inner row is 59 px so the header with its 1 px border is 60 px, as drawn | Pixel QA found the page content 1 px low on every screen |
