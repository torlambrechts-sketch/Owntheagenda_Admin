# Deviations — Growth v3 (GrowthMarketing.dc.html of 12 Oct 2026)

Where the build differs from the drawing, and why. The element-by-element map of sources is `../v3/growth-v3-gap.md`; the
earlier Growth deviations (V-SGR-*, V-GR2-*) stay in force where not replaced here. Migration `0083_growth_v3.sql`; rules in
`lib/growth/v3.ts` (tested in `tests/v3-growth.test.ts`); proofs in `supabase/tests/v3_growth_invariants.sql`.

| ID | Where | Design | Built | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| V-V3G-01 | Campaign card › Daily spend and signups | 21 random spend bars for every campaign | Spend bars only from the ad platforms' days (`ad_daily`, the platform campaigns that count as this one). A campaign with typed spend, no spend or accounts in several currencies draws its signups bars alone, with one line saying why («Spend is entered per month; daily spend comes from a connected ad platform»); with no signups either, every day is the design's 2 % stub | Spend is typed per month and never spread over days (V-SGR-09, R-2); currencies are never converted (V-GR2-43) |
| V-V3G-02 | Daily bars | A bar on every day, before the start and while paused | A day before the campaign's start is an empty slot (its tooltip says so); so is a day after its end, unless the platform recorded spend or someone signed up that day (a platform campaign can run past the end date Sentral holds; its link keeps bringing people) — its tooltip says «after the campaign ended». When the campaign ended before the 21 days began and nothing was recorded in them, one line replaces the bars: «The campaign ended {date}; none of the last 21 days falls within it». A platform day with no row and no signups is an empty slot; a zero-spend day is the 2 % stub | A bar where nothing ran would be invented |
| V-V3G-03 | Daily bars › signups | Random | The website tracker's completed signups (`signup_completed`) on visits carrying the campaign's `utm_campaign`, by the Oslo day they finished; a line under the bars names the source. The card's existing «Signups» stat (`ad_campaigns_get`) still counts product attribution, so the two can differ until D-GR3-2 is decided (R-1: Orgpuls sends only `heard`) | D-GR3-2 option (a), the one source live now; the stat is not this wave's to change |
| V-V3G-04 | Daily bars › window and labels | Ends «11 Oct», month hard-coded «Oct» | The 21 days to yesterday (Europe/Oslo); «Sun 11 Oct» from the date itself, so September reads «Mon 21 Sept». Platform days are the ad account's own; when an account is not on Oslo time a line says so | The design's labels are wrong across a month; platforms report in the account's time zone (R-10) |
| V-V3G-05 | Day line | «Sat 10 Oct: spent 423 kr, 3 signups, 141 kr per signup» | The same words; «spent —» where spend has no days; «per signup» only when both spend and signups exist. A click on the day again closes it; each day is a button with `aria-pressed` and a label (keyboard) | Never a cost over an unknown spend |
| V-V3G-06 | Ads and keywords in this campaign | Literal lists per channel, including «Registrations» (Webinar) and «Main CTA» (Newsletter) | Shown only for a platform-linked campaign: its Google keywords (imported read only from `keyword_view`, 0083) then its ads (`ad_platform_ads`), each up to six by clicks in the same 21 days (the read returns up to 20 of each; the card shows six), with clicks, signups and cost per signup. Webinar and newsletter rows are not shown (no source: D-GR3-7, D-GR3-8); Meta ad sets are not imported, so Meta shows its ads. Column headers are visually hidden (screen readers have them); two muted lines name the window and the tracking convention | Each row is a record that exists, with its own figures |
| V-V3G-07 | Signups per ad and keyword | Literal | By convention (D-GR3-3): `utm_term` = the keyword (Google `{keyword}`), `utm_content` = the ad's id (Google `{creative}`, Meta `{{ad.id}}`, LinkedIn's creative id). When none of the campaign's visits in the window carries the parameter, the column is «—» and a line says what the tracking template needs | Sentral can only count what the links carry; a share of the campaign's signups by clicks would be invented (R-3) |
| V-V3G-08 | Cost per signup per row | Campaign spend split by click share | The row's own imported spend over its own signups; «—» without signups or spend; red over 900 kr as drawn, for kroner only | R-3 |
| V-V3G-09 | Campaign tips | Literal texts per channel («costs 4× more», «raise its bid 20 %», «1 300 kr per signup» against a row of 1 135 kr) | Rules on the rows' own figures, at most two per campaign, same currency only (D-GR3-9, the builder's stated rules): **dear** — a keyword or ad with ≥ 50 clicks whose cost per signup is ≥ 3× the others' together (the ratio printed), or that has no signups after spending more than a signup costs on the others; **best** — a keyword with ≥ 3 signups at ≤ half the others' cost per signup, only under manual or enhanced CPC (`campaign.bidding_strategy_type`, imported in 0083; smart bidding sets bids itself); no percentage is suggested. The rules run over the rows the card shows (six of each kind), so a tip never names a row outside the list and «the rest» is the rest of the list | A claim must rest on the figures shown |
| V-V3G-10 | Tip buttons («Pause keyword», «Raise bid», «Move budget») | Buttons; the tip vanishes and a toast says «· applied via <channel> API» | Links that open the place in Google Ads, LinkedIn Campaign Manager or Meta Ads Manager where the change is made: «Pause in Google Ads ↗», «Raise bid in Google Ads ↗», «Open in LinkedIn Ads ↗»; the title says Sentral changes nothing there. The tip stays while its rule holds | X-V3-3: no writes to the ad platforms; a toast claiming one would be fabricated (R-4). Control substitution: a button that navigates becomes a link |
| V-V3G-11 | Tips not shown | «Add exclusion» (LinkedIn CRM list), «Limit audience» (Meta), «Enroll» (Webinar), «Set time» (Newsletter) | Not shown | Uploading CRM lists awaits D-GR3-4 (V-GR2-45); Meta ad sets are not imported; webinar attendees and the newsletter's email link await D-GR3-7 and D-GR3-8 |
| V-V3G-12 | Best time to send | A formula, the same for every sequence; «Best: Tue 08:00 (54 %)» literal | The application's email (every campaign of the application whose reports the viewer may see and that tracks opens), the last 90 days, by send weekday × hour in Oslo time: human opens over delivered. A cell needs 20 delivered. «Best:» (Norwegian «Best tid:») is the highest open rate among those (ties: more delivered); the window is that hour on every weekday whose cell at it has 20 delivered and a rate within five points of the best. «Best: —» until a cell qualifies | D-GR3-6 defaults (PLAN.md: 90 days, Oslo, 20 sends); a single sequence rarely has 20 sends in a cell |
| V-V3G-13 | Heatmap cells | Four fills for every cell; Tue 08 always bordered | Fills banded against the best cell as the design bands its formula (> 85 %, > 60 %, > 40 % of the best); a cell under 20 delivered has no fill, only a dashed outline (its tooltip says how many sends it has), never «low»; the yellow border only on a qualifying best cell; sends at hours outside the twelve columns are counted in a line under the grid. The legend's three swatches for four fills are kept as drawn. The grid is a table for screen readers and scrolls inside the card when narrow | A low fill would claim a low rate (R-8); V-SGR-27 at 390 px |
| V-V3G-14 | Results per step | `enrolled × 3 − i × 30`, fixed rate arrays, signups `sent × click × 0,3`, rates printed over 0 sent | Per email step, the last 30 days (`campaign_recipients` by `sent_at`): sent, open and click rates over delivered (human opens; «—» when the email does not track opens), unsubscribes over delivered, and signups = the tracker's visits from the email's links (`utm_campaign`) that finished signing up in the window. An email that sent nothing: «0» and «—»; another person's email without Campaigns › See reports of others: «—». A line says Apple's automatic opens are not counted. The table scrolls inside the card under 400 px | Never a rate over nothing (R-8); D-GR3-2 option (a) |
| V-V3G-15 | «Create A/B test» | Removes the tip; toast «· done»; the tip proposes «Last opp 48 ansatte på 2 minutter» | Shown when an email has ≥ 20 delivered and a click rate under 10 % (its number and rate real); no example subject; the button is off with its reason in sight («an automated email cannot have an A/B subject») | The schema refuses subject B on automated campaigns (V-P6C-11, D-GR3-5); the example subject is invented copy |
| V-V3G-16 | «Add condition» | Removes the tip | Shown when an email after the first has ≥ 20 delivered sends to people with an organisation, a quarter or more of which had uploaded their employee list before the send (the share printed), and no «employee list is not uploaded» condition stands between it and the email before. The button opens the editor at that email: a bar right above it offers «＋ Condition here» with that condition, saved through the editor (`simple_sequence_save`, its rights); nothing changes until it is pressed | The simple model's condition ends the run (V-SGR-20) rather than skipping one email, and the bar says so; an automatic edit would change the sequence without the person seeing it |
| V-V3G-17 | «Use best time», «Send all steps at the best time» | Local state; toast «Steps now send Tue–Thu at 08:00»; no step changes | One per-sequence setting (`sequence_send_windows`, none by default), derived in the database from the best time when switched on; the button reads «✓ Sending Tue 08:00» with the stored window and switches it off again. The engine honours it at every email step (`wf_step`'s campaign_action, 0083): reached outside the window, the run waits for the next slot (Oslo's clock, across clock changes) and asks «stop when» again then. The first email waits too. Off with its reason while there is no best time, no email, or no right to edit; when the send times could not be read, the reason says that («Needs the send times, which could not be read»), never that no time has 20 sends | The gap proposed a delay overload; a hold at the email step covers the first email (D-GR3-6) and leaves every existing delay unchanged (R-11). Proven in SQL |
| V-V3G-18 | Toasts | «<cta> · done» | What was saved: «Steps now send Tue at 08:00», «Steps send when each wait ends», «Condition added before email 2», or the refusal — each only after the database confirmed the save (the condition's toast waits for the editor's queued save) | Never a toast that pretends |
