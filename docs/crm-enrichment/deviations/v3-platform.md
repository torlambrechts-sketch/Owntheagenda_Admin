# Sentral v3 › Platform, the new tabs: deviations

Design: `design-reference/sentral-simple/PlatformPlus.dc.html` (views `ops`, `billing`, `success`, `access`) and the tab strip of
`SimplePlatform.dc.html`. Gap analysis: `docs/crm-enrichment/v3/platform-gap.md`. Route `/platform?tab=ops|billing|success|access`
(and `?tab=team`), components in `app/(admin)/platform/_components/plus/`, actions in `app/(admin)/platform/plus-actions.ts`,
loaders in `lib/data/platform-plus.ts`, migration `0082_platform_plus.sql` (suite `supabase/tests/v3_platform_invariants.sql`),
unit tests `tests/v3-platform.test.ts`. Decisions used: X-V3-4 (today's five roles), D-30 (approvals off by default), D-22,
D-40, D-41 and the defaults in `v3/PLAN.md` (D-PL-06, D-PL-07, D-PL-09, D-PL-15, D-PL-16).

| ID | Where | Design | Built | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| V-V3P-01 | Tab strip | Seven pill `<button>`s switching local state | The same pills as links: `/platform`, `?tab=ops`, `?tab=plans`, `?tab=billing`, `?tab=success`, `?tab=team`, `?tab=access`; «Team and roles» is «Team» | Control substitution (V-SPL-01): a tab has an address |
| V-V3P-02 | Roles: columns | Super-admin · Support · Billing · Ops · Sales · Read-only | Admin · Sales · Support · Marketing · Read-only, the permission sets Team offers, in Team's order | X-V3-4: today's five roles; Billing and Ops do not exist |
| V-V3P-03 | Roles: cells | A literal ✓/A/– pattern per row (Delete account «A» for Super-admin, …) | Each cell is the role's real policy, seeded from today's rights: allowed where the role holds the action's right, else not allowed; no «A» anywhere until someone switches approval on (D-30). Changing a cell takes effect for that role, fresh second factor, audited with before and after. The role's rights are the ceiling: the matrix narrows a role (✓ → A → –), never widens it; a cell the role's rights do not reach stays «–», disabled, its title naming the right to give in Settings › Roles (or «Only the Admin role can do this»), the footer says so, and the database denies such a cell whatever a policy row holds | No one gains or loses a right by the migration; the design's pattern is example data; a matrix that could grant Read-only «Delete account» would be a second, weaker rights system |
| V-V3P-04 | Roles: View accounts, Reply to tickets, Request support access, Export customer lists | Clickable cells | The cell shows the role's right (Global › See, Tickets › Add, Global › Add, Deals › Export) and is not clickable; its title says the right is changed in Settings › Roles | Those actions are decided by rights inside each function; a policy here that nothing enforced would be cosmetic |
| V-V3P-05 | Roles: Change plan or price, Refund or credit note, Discount over 20 % | ✓/A/– cells | «–» for every role, disabled, «not available yet» after the label | No plan command (`billing.change_plan`), no credit-note register (D-PL-10), no discount in Orgpuls (D-PL-11) |
| V-V3P-06 | Roles: «A» | Any cell cycles ✓ → A → – | «A» is offered for Extend trial, Delete account or user data and Manage admin roles, the actions the queue can carry out; Change feature flags and Publish announcements cycle ✓ ↔ – (enforced in product-command). While Settings › Second admin is on, Delete account cannot be ✓: an allowed cell reads «A», cycles A ↔ –, its title and a line under the matrix say why, and product-command's gate asks for the approval too | Their requests would come from Applications, which has no request path yet; an «A» nobody can ask for would block the role. D-30: one rule, enforced by the database, not by one screen |
| V-V3P-07 | Roles: footnote | «Super-admin and Billing require two-factor login with a security key» | «Every sign-in needs a second factor · all changes are logged · click a cell to change it» (the last part only for people who may change the matrix) | TOTP only (D-11); security keys wait for D-PL-03 |
| V-V3P-08 | Roles: Manage admin roles | Governs role management | Governs changes to this matrix (and who may approve them). Team's role select still follows Global › Security | Team's content is unchanged in this handoff; lead request to ask roles.manage there |
| V-V3P-09 | Queue rows | Refund 3 390 kr · Delete organization · Discount 35 % · Export all contacts (4 120) | Real requests only: a trial extension (account, days), a deletion (account, org.nr typed), a change to the matrix (action, role, from → to). Amount column «+14 days» or «—». Refunds, discounts and exports cannot be requested | No credit notes, no discounts; an export is built in the browser (nothing for an approval to run) |
| V-V3P-10 | Queue: Approve | Marks «✓ Approved by Tor», toasts «Approved · action executed and logged» | Approve (fresh second factor, never one's own request, only someone who may take the action) runs the action at once: a matrix change in the database; a product command by the approver through product-command, bound to the approval (`command_authorize`: exactly the approved arguments, once, within five minutes), with the outcome recorded. The row reads «✓ Approved by {decider}» when it ran, «Failed (code)» with the product's code when it did not; the toast says which. When the app gets no answer it can read (40 s, a page that is not product-command's), the command audit decides: the call ran → executed; nothing went out → failed; no answer in the audit yet → «Approved by {decider} · outcome not confirmed yet» until the sweep settles it from the audit within ten minutes | The approval must execute, never pretend to (R-PL-1, R-PL-6) |
| V-V3P-11 | Queue: requesting | Not drawn | «＋ Request approval» in the queue's header for people whose role needs approval for an action the queue carries out: account, days or the org.nr, and a reason | D-41: a queue nobody can add to is a dead end (R-PL-13); Customers' own action menu still answers «needs approval» (lead request) |
| V-V3P-12 | Queue: deletion | One-click Approve | The approver types the org.nr again before Approve (D-30, R-PL-13) | A deletion cannot be undone |
| V-V3P-13 | Queue: expiry | Not drawn | A request expires after seven days («Expired»); an approval not carried out within ten minutes ends as «Failed (not_taken)». The argument values are cleared when a request runs, is rejected or expires — swept every five minutes by the job monitor (`approval_sweep`, cron `platform-approval-sweep`) and at each write to the queue | Rule R-PL-6. The job's label in Applications › Job monitor (`platform.jobLabels.approval_sweep`, the shared `platform.json`) is a lead request; until then the monitor shows its key |
| V-V3P-14 | Rollouts card | Four invented rollouts with stage bars, errors and adoption; «Rolls back automatically if the error rate doubles» | The card with one line: Orgpuls has no feature flags that can be staged per organisation; the question modules are under Applications › Feature flags. No stage bar, no Widen / Roll back / Restart, no automatic roll-back line | `flags.list`, `flags.set_stage`, `flags.daily` do not exist in Orgpuls (D-PL-04); no guard runs (D-PL-05) |
| V-V3P-15 | Announcement composer | Prefilled «Nytt: pulsundersøkelser …»; chips All products · Orgpuls · Admins · Trial; «Publish · 372 users» (1240 × 0,3) | Empty text with a placeholder; chips: the product (fixed: the only reachable one), Admins (= the daglig leder of each account, D-PL-06 default), Trial; «All products» not drawn. The reach is real: active customer users of live accounts, narrowed by the chips. Publish is disabled, with the reason as its title and on a line below | No in-app announcement command (`announcements.publish`); Settings › status message reaches the public site only (F-P2) |
| V-V3P-16 | Accounts near a plan limit | Employees, admins, SMS per month, storage; «Notify about band», «Raise to …» that toast; «Raised · 58 %» | Employees only: the latest `usage.daily` employee count against the plan's limit (by plan key, F-P1), at 80 % or more, red from 95 %. «Notify about band» disabled with its reason; nothing is raised | D-PL-07: headcount is the only limit Orgpuls has; notifying and raising need Orgpuls' flow; «58 %» is invented arithmetic |
| V-V3P-17 | Errors per customer | Application error logs and invitation deferrals per customer, a «No errors» row, «Ticket created with the error log attached» | Sentral's own email to the customer that failed in 24 h: ticket replies not delivered, campaign mail bounced or failed — counts only. A line counts the accounts without errors and says the product's own error counts are not sent yet. «Create ticket» opens a bug ticket whose note carries the counts (read again on the server), once while it is open; «✓ Ticket open» links to it | No `errors.hourly` from Orgpuls; invitation mail is Orgpuls' (R-PL-3); a log can carry a respondent's words (R-PL-2) |
| V-V3P-18 | Onboarding checklist | Checkbox per step, «Untick a step to hide it from the in-app checklist», «Shown in the app» | No checkboxes, no footnote; «Completion by step» for the accounts created in the last 180 days (a line says how many). From `usage.daily`, members (Invite HR and leaders = a second customer user) and the milestones Orgpuls reports; a step with no source for the product is «—», not 0 % | Hiding steps in the product needs Orgpuls' onboarding commands (D-PL-13); `results_viewed` and `first_tiltak` come only as `milestone.reached` (D-21) |
| V-V3P-19 | NPS card | «+42», «214 answers · +5 vs last quarter», the three-part bar, «Asked in the app every 90 days», the detractor line | «—» in muted grey (the design's green is for a good result) and «No source: Orgpuls does not ask its customers for a score yet» | Customer NPS has no source (Orgpuls 0141; D-PL-12); eNPS is respondent data and never comes here (R-PL-4) |
| V-V3P-20 | Feature requests | ▲ adds one local vote; «8 680 kr MRR asking» (mrr × 620); «When a request is set to Shipped, everyone who voted gets an email and the changelog is updated» | ▲ shows the votes and opens a dialog to record a vote on behalf of an organisation (one per organisation, 0078); tickets and deals are the linked ones; the MRR asking is the paid MRR of the accounts behind votes, tickets and deals — only for people with Deals › See, «—» when none of them carries a price; status select with 0078's statuses («Not planned» = declined); footnote «Setting a request to Shipped emails no one yet»; «＋ Add request» added | Real requests only; no voter e-mail or changelog link exists (D-PL-14); D-41 for adding |
| V-V3P-21 | Renewal and expansion | «Next 60 days»; Bergheim «will cross into 101–250»; Skiftly trial / shift-staff cross-sell rows; «Logins −60 % · renews in 34 days» | «From usage, health and sign-ins»: within 5 employees of the plan's limit (Expansion: «{n} employees · {max} is the plan's limit (next band {next})», a position, not a forecast); health down 20 points against 30 days earlier, or no sign-in for 30 days (At risk). Cross-sell rows not drawn | No renewal date or login trend from Orgpuls (`current_period_end`, `logins.daily`); other products are not connected (D-PL-17) |
| V-V3P-22 | Create deal / CSM task | Toasts and «✓ Created» | A real deal on the account (the first pipeline, source «Customer success») or a task for the account's customer success manager (else its owner, else the person clicking), due tomorrow; «✓ Created» while that deal is open or the task not done. Needs Deals › Add | D-PL-15 defaults |
| V-V3P-23 | Billing portal: header | «Self-service billing · Lumio AS», «What the customer sees in Innstillinger → Abonnement», «Preview» chip | «Billing · {account}», «The plan Orgpuls bills this account for», the subscription's status as the chip, and an account picker (D-41) | D-PL-09 default: an operations view, no mock of the customer's page |
