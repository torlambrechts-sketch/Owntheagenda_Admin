# Customers › Help centre and support: deviations from the design

Design: `design-reference/sentral-simple/SupportHub.dc.html` and the third pill in `SimpleCustomers.dc.html` (route
`/customers?tab=support&sub=metrics|widget|center|channels|portal|assist|proactive`). Gap analysis:
`docs/crm-enrichment/v3/support-gap.md`. Migration `supabase/migrations/0081_support_hub.sql`, proved by
`supabase/tests/v3_support_invariants.sql` and `tests/v3-support.test.ts`. Edge function `csat` (new); `mail-run` and
`mail-events` extended for the CSAT request and the inbound fixes.

0081 fixes two production defects first, each with a SQL test that failed before the fix:
- **F-S1** — Orgpuls' `contact.submitted` topic was ignored and its in-app request's words (`body`) were dropped. Topics route
  through `app.contact_topic_routes` (product → Getting started, pricing → Sales, first_send → Survey delivery, privacy →
  personvern); a ticket in the personvern category gets its `privacy_requests` row (the 30-day clock, from the moment the
  customer sent it). Only a contact routed to the Sales queue makes a CRM lead, titled by the form's subject or the category's
  name and never by the customer's message: a lead is read under `deals:see`, by roles that may not see the ticket's queue
  (rule 6). A support topic (product, first send) or a privacy request makes no lead.
- **F-S2** — an auto-reply reopened a resolved ticket. Inbound mail matches `In-Reply-To` / `References` against the Message-ID
  of the reply Sentral sent before the `[T-n]` subject. An auto-reply by its headers (`Auto-Submitted`,
  `Precedence: auto_reply|bulk|junk|list`, `X-Autoreply`, `X-Autorespond`, `X-Auto-Response-Suppress`) is never threaded and
  never reopens (audited as `ticket.auto_reply_ignored`). A subject in the auto-reply shape — a known prefix (Norwegian,
  Swedish, English, German) followed by a colon, «Automatisk svar: …», «Fravær: …» — is only a guess: the message is kept in
  the thread, the ticket is not reopened and nobody is notified (`ticket.auto_reply_kept`). A subject that merely begins with
  «Fravær» or «Ikke til stede» is a customer's and is handled as one. When the database refuses an inbound answer, mail-events
  answers 500 so Brevo delivers it again (a repeat is a duplicate by its inbound id); the log counts `failed` with codes only.
